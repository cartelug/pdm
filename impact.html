Institutional milestones

Seven pieces of evidence.

01

Pamodzi 4 Development (P4D) — Kasese District

A community-driven development platform bringing together members with a shared vision of improving livelihoods through collective action and mutual support. Members established an internal lending mechanism giving affordable access to finance for business growth, agriculture, education and household development.

55Members

02

Udada Women Entrepreneurs Network — Wakiso District

Mobilised within Akright City, bringing together women entrepreneurs across diverse sectors. The network promotes financial inclusion, entrepreneurship and business networking, strengthening women's participation in local economic development through collaborative investment and peer support.

50Women entrepreneurs

03

TBS Meridian Realities LLP

A private investment platform for collective wealth creation through disciplined capital mobilisation and strategic asset acquisition, later partnered with Pieteck Limited. Investments to date include two hotel suites and two condominium units — an initial portfolio of income-generating real estate.

Business Legends Group (BLG)

A regional private investment initiative focused on hospitality and commercial investment across East and Southern Africa, developed in strategic partnership with Bloktavia Estates. 05

Walking for Impact — the Faith in Motion campaign

An innovative community resource mobilisation model in which every sponsored step taken by a designated champion is assigned a monetary value. Delivered in partnership with the Rotary Club of Akright City and the Walking Rotarian, in support of St Joseph Catholic Church at Rwembyo, Kisinga, Kasese District.

06

Community medical outreach — Nyabirongo Health Centre III

PCI Uganda convened the Rotary Club of Akright City, the Ministry of Health through the Kasese District Health Office, healthcare professionals and other stakeholders for a community medical camp in Kisinga Town Council. The camp provided free consultations, health education, disease screening, laboratory tests, counselling, treatment and selected medical supplies.
