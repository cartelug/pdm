What happens during review

The concept moves through definition, governance and feasibility checks before Pamodzi decides whether it is ready for a public project pathway.

Step 01 · Define

Clarify the community need

Document the housing need, intended users, location context and the practical outcome the concept is meant to support.

Step 02 · Review

Test feasibility and governance

Review delivery options, responsibilities, approvals, legal requirements and safeguards before any public commitment is invited.

Step 03 · Consult

Listen to relevant stakeholders

Gather practical input from the community and appropriately qualified advisers, then revise the concept around evidence.

Step 04 · Decide

Publish only an approved pathway

If the concept proceeds, Pamodzi will publish its verified status and the appropriate route for questions or participation.
