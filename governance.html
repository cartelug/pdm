Pamodzi / Governance

Clear rules.
Responsible support.

Transparency, accountability and responsible stewardship are the conditions for any public contribution pathway.

The rules

Five that never bend.

The same rules govern every project that opens under Pamodzi — not as a promise, but as how the system is built.

RULE 01

Beneficiary first

Every contribution pathway must identify the approved beneficiary and transfer instructions. A campaign page or message alone never authorises a payment.

RULE 02

Only reconciled figures

No total is published until it has been checked against the relevant source records. Nothing is shown as received until it is verified.

RULE 03

No guaranteed outcomes

We never promise a fundraising result or an investment return. The system exists to remove friction and build trust, not to make predictions.

RULE 04

Approval before publication

Money figures, beneficiary details and named individuals require review and permission before anything becomes public.

RULE 05

Verify every channel

When payment details are not published, contributors request the current approved instructions and verify the beneficiary before sending.

Follow the money

From a supporter to a finished building.

Every contribution takes the same path, and contributions never pass through the campaign team at any point.

Communication separation

Different things, kept different.

Community development work and investment activity are not the same transaction, and we never present them as though they were.

Grants, donations, sponsorships, membership contributions and any commercial arrangement are distinct and require their own documentation. No investment or financial terms are offered on this website; any future offer would require separate legal, financial and governance review.

Anti-fraud. If a project page does not publish payment details, do not treat a number or account received elsewhere as approved. Request the current instructions through the listed enquiry contact and verify the beneficiary before sending.
