PCI Uganda / Governance

Easy to support.
Impossible to misuse.

Transparency, accountability and responsible stewardship are not a section of the strategy — they are the condition that makes collective investment possible at all.

The rules

Five that never bend.

The same rules govern every project that opens under PCI Uganda — not as a promise, but as how the system is built.

RULE 01

Funds go direct

Contributions go straight to the formally authorised beneficiary account — a church construction account, a community platform, or another approved account. The people who build and run these pages never receive or hold a donation.

RULE 02

Only reconciled figures

No total is published until the designated finance verifier has confirmed it. Nothing is shown as raised until it is verified.

RULE 03

No guaranteed outcomes

We never promise a fundraising result or an investment return. The system exists to remove friction and build trust, not to make predictions.

RULE 04

Approval before publication

Money figures, beneficiary details and named individuals pass through the content approver before anything becomes public.

RULE 05

One official channel

Every giving surface names the official payment channel, so an impersonator is easy to spot. Any other request for money in our name is not from us.

Follow the money

From a supporter to a finished building.

Every contribution takes the same path, and contributions never pass through the campaign team at any point.

Communication separation

Different things, kept different.

Community development work and investment activity are not the same transaction, and we never present them as though they were.

Donations, sponsorships, membership contributions, community fundraising, commercial investment, founding-partner contributions and development financing each carry their own terms, their own documentation and their own legal description. Investment language, equity participation and any founding-capital framework receive legal, financial and governance review before publication.

Anti-fraud. If someone asks you to send money for a PCI Uganda project through a channel not published on this site, it is not from us. Check the campaign page for the official number before you send anything.
