Pamodzi / Governance

Rules that never bend.

Every Pamodzi project runs on the same system: money moves directly, figures are reconciled before they are published, and named people approve what goes public.

How we work

Easy to support. Impossible to misuse.

The same rules govern every project that opens under Pamodzi — not as a promise, but as how the system is built.

01

Funds go direct

Contributions go straight to the authorised account of the church, Pamodzi, or the named beneficiary. The people who build and run these pages never receive or hold a donation.

02

Only confirmed figures

Totals published here are reconciled and approved by the designated finance verifier. Nothing is shown as raised until it is verified.

03

Approved before public

Money figures, beneficiary details and named individuals pass through a content approver before they appear. No outcome is ever guaranteed.

Anti-fraud

One official channel.

For the live Faith in Motion campaign, the only published collection line is Mobile Money 0772 495 733 — Sylvia Kaawe, Chair of the Construction Committee. Any other request for money in the campaign's name is not from us: do not pay it, and report it to the contacts on this site.
