Areas of practice

Community priorities taking shape.

  1. 01

    Mutual support and member wellbeing

    Members organise practical support around education, medical, household and livelihood needs, combining solidarity with clear records and shared responsibility.

  2. 02

    Education and school development

    Pamodzi’s education work focuses on locally identified school needs, learning environments and the partnerships required to turn a defined priority into a deliverable project.

  3. 03

    Faith and community infrastructure

    Defined community-building needs can be supported through project-specific authorisation, direct beneficiary channels and reporting that continues until the work is complete.

    See Faith in Motion See Walk for Education
  4. 04

    Health and community wellbeing

    The health pillar is designed around community priorities, practical health education and collaboration with appropriately authorised health professionals and public bodies. A community medical camp convened with the Rotary Club of Akright City and the Kasese District Health Office at Nyabirongo Health Centre III, Kisinga, on 10 June 2023, reached over 600 people with free consultations, screening and treatment.

  5. 05

    Enterprise and financial inclusion

    Community savings, financial literacy, peer support and enterprise development help members strengthen livelihoods while building the discipline needed for larger shared initiatives.

Since 2022

Platforms we’ve helped establish.

Beyond campaigns like Faith in Motion, Pamodzi has convened members and partners into legally distinct, member-owned platforms. These are savings, investment and enterprise structures — not donation channels — governed and financed by their own members.

55 committed members Pamodzi 4 Development (P4D), Kasese District
50 women entrepreneurs Udada Women Entrepreneurs Network, Wakiso District
16 investment partners TBS Meridian Realities LLP
  1. 01

    Pamodzi 4 Development (P4D) — Kasese District

    A community-driven platform bringing together 55 committed members around collective savings, an internal lending mechanism and mutual support for business, agriculture, education and household needs.

  2. 02

    Udada Women Entrepreneurs Network — Wakiso District

    A network of 50 women entrepreneurs within Akright City, built around enterprise financing, business networking and peer support for women’s economic empowerment.

  3. 03

    TBS Meridian Realities LLP

    A member-owned real estate investment platform mobilised with 16 investment partners, with holdings already established in hotel suites and condominium units.

  4. 04

    Business Legends Group (BLG)

    A regional private investment initiative expanding hospitality and commercial ventures across East and Southern Africa, in partnership with Bloktavia Estates.

Member platforms, not public donations. These structures are financed and owned by their own members and investment partners. Consolidated mobilisation figures exist in Pamodzi’s internal institutional record and will be published here once independently verified.
