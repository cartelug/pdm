Walking now · arrives 2 August 2026

345 kilometres on foot to finish a church.

Faith in Motion is Pamodzi's live campaign. The Walking Rotarian is crossing the country from Kampala to Kasese — 466,200 steps — to complete St Joseph Rwembyo Catholic Church. Every step can be sponsored for UGX 1,000, and every sponsor is named on a public roll.
