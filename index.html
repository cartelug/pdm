Who we are

Not a donor. A convener.

Most development money arrives from outside and leaves when the project ends. We work the other way round: organising the resources a community already has — savings groups, cooperatives, professionals, businesses, diaspora, local government — into investments large enough to build something permanent.

PCI Uganda works alongside Government ministries, district local governments, financial institutions, development partners and community structures to deliver infrastructure, institutional capacity and community empowerment programmes across the country.

Sustainable development is most effective when communities become active investors in their own future.
