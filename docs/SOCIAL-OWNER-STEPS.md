# Social access: owner steps

No social-platform role was granted or verified by this website release. Before sending any invitation, verify the exact person's profile/email with PCI and the person, confirm the correct PCI asset, and choose the least authority needed. Staff accept invitations with their own accounts; never share a password in website code.

- **Facebook:** the owner with full-control Page access switches to the PCI Page, opens Settings → Page setup → Page access, adds the verified person and grants the appropriate Facebook/task access. Confirm acceptance in Page access before reporting completion. [Official Page access instructions](https://www.facebook.com/help/187316341316631).
- **Instagram:** the owner uses the linked PCI business portfolio in Meta Business Suite → Settings → People → Invite people, verifies the email, and assigns only the correct Instagram asset and required content/message tasks. Confirm the linked account and accepted invitation in the owner’s actual interface; available controls depend on account configuration. [Meta access reference](https://www.facebook.com/help/289207354498410/).
- **TikTok:** Business Center admin invites the verified member under Users/Members, then assigns the PCI TikTok asset and supported permissions under Assets → TikTok accounts. Review the actual permissions: ad delivery authority alone does not establish organic posting access. Confirm the accepted invitation and a permitted action before treating setup as complete. [Official member instructions](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/add-users-tiktok-business-center?lang=en) and [account permissions](https://ads.us.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-grant-members-access-to-tiktok-accounts-in-business-center?lang=en).
- **X:** on X.com, the owner opens Settings and privacy → Security and account access → Delegate → Invite a member, verifies the exact account and chooses Contributor unless broader authority is required. The member accepts the invitation in Delegate; owner confirms the member and role. [Official Delegate instructions](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/how-to-use-the-delegate-feature).

After acceptance, verify a permitted draft/read action on the correct PCI account. Record who approved the role and who accepted it. Remove access when a person leaves and review periodically. These instructions do not themselves grant any website or finance permission.
