# Guiding Joshua — your 15-minute script

Everything on the website is already live on `main`. The only things that need a human are the three below.

## Before the call (do once, ~10 minutes)

- [ ] **Accounts are activated.** The host owner opens cPanel Terminal in the website root and runs `php scripts/setup-operations-user.php` (the helper ships in https://pamodzici.com/assets/operations/setup/PCI_Account_Setup.zip — extract it in the website root first). Run it once each for:
  - Joshua → role `coordinator`
  - You (Neeza) → role `coordinator`
  - The finance verifier → role `finance`
  Passwords: 14+ characters, personal, typed twice (hidden). Nothing is printed or stored in git.
- [ ] **Send Joshua his login privately** (not in the group chat, not in the same message as the guide).
- [ ] **Send the guide:** paste `SEND-TO-JOSHUA.txt` into WhatsApp and attach `PCI-Joshua-Guide.pdf`.
- [ ] Quick self-test: log in as a coordinator — you should see Record contribution, Update walk status, Generate graphic, Download report, and **no** "Verify & reconcile".

No account is active until someone does step 1. Nothing here has been activated for you.

## On the call

| Min | Say | Joshua does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 | "Open the link and sign in. Find the four buttons." | Signs in, spots the four buttons |
| 2–4 | "A contribution is recorded once. Saved = waiting for finance." | Does **not** enter fake data. Just opens the form and points to each field |

| 6–8 | "Walk updates only use facts the walker confirmed." | Opens Update walk status; saves nothing |
| 8–12 | "Practice graphic: SAMPLE DATA → Collections snapshot → Feed → preview → Download." | Downloads the PNG |
| 12–15 | "Open the public Walk page. Sign out." | Signs out |

## Three things to stress

1. Promised, received, steps, kilometres are four different numbers.
2. Joshua never marks money received. Finance does.
3. His login is his. No sharing, no posting it in chats.

- [ ] Joshua signed in on his own phone
- [ ] He downloaded a SAMPLE DATA graphic
- [ ] He knows to ask finance, not re-enter, when unsure
