# Joshua's guide — PCI campaign operations

**Your link (save it to your phone's home screen):** https://pamodzici.com/admin/operations/
**Start page with this same walkthrough:** https://pamodzici.com/admin/operations/start.html

You have your own login. Never share it. Sign out when you finish.

## What you can do (coordinator)

| Button | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Record contribution | Enter a reported payment once |
| Update walk status | Publish the confirmed town, date, kilometres, story and photo |
| Generate graphic | Make an approved picture to share |
| Download report | Get the records as a file |

You will **not** see "Verify & reconcile". That is finance on purpose. You can never mark money as received.

## Your 10-minute practice

1. **Sign in** (minutes 0–2). Find the four buttons above.
2. **Learn how to record a contribution** (2–4). Use a real receipt you are authorised to record; if you have none, look at the fields but do not save a practice entry. Supporter, amount in whole numbers, currency, actual payment date, channel, reference. Save. Today's date and the published step price fill in for you. For a part-payment, pick the related pledge.
3. **Understand "saved"** (4–5). Saved means waiting for finance. Only after finance checks the real bank or mobile-money record does it count as received on the public site. A pledge or a screenshot is not proof.
4. **Prepare a walk update** (5–7). Use only facts the walker has confirmed: town, date, total kilometres, a short story, an approved photo. Preview, then publish. Not ready? Save draft.
5. **Make a practice graphic** (7–9). Generate graphic → **SAMPLE DATA** → Collections snapshot → Feed → Create graphic preview → Download PNG, then open it on your phone and check the logos, words and numbers are all readable. Sample data touches no real records. For real sharing choose **Approved live snapshot**.
6. **Check and sign out** (9–10). Open the public Walk page, find the dated update, find Download report, sign out.

**Practice rule:** rehearse with SAMPLE DATA only. Never enter fake donations or an invented walk status in the live register.

## Every day

1. A contribution is reported → you record it **once** with the correct reference. Online (Pesapal) payments are already in their own ledger; ask finance, do not re-enter them.
2. Finance verifies and reconciles.
3. The walker sends an approved update → you publish it.
4. End of day → review pending entries with finance, download the report, make an approved graphic if needed. Only name supporters who gave permission.

Keep these four separate, always: **promised support · verified money · sponsored steps · kilometres walked.**

## If something is wrong

- Do not record it twice. Search the reference and ask finance to review.
- Saved the wrong amount or reference? Give finance the entry; they correct it with an audit trail. Never add a second entry to cancel it.
- A supporter withdraws permission to be named? Ask finance to update consent, and remove any graphic already shared.
- Unsure of a town or kilometre figure? Save a draft and wait for confirmation.
- If the page will not load, wait and retry. It does **not** mean collections are zero.
- Wrong login or no login yet → tell Neeza; only the hosting administrator can activate accounts.
