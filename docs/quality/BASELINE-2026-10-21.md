### Speed

The "ceiling" is the budget in `tests/quality/budget.json`: the 17 October results plus about 15%. It is **not** a measurement. The platform plan's "now" column quotes the ceilings, so its figure of 4.3 s for the Walk home and 4.4 s for Faith in Motion are ceilings. These are measured values:

| Page | LCP ms (ceiling) | KB (ceiling) | Blocking ms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | 1916 (2300) | 309 (360) | 580 |
| `/about/` | 2512 (3050) | 209 (240) | 349 |
| `/projects/` | 3040 (3450) | 327 (375) | 242 |
| `/walk-for-education/` | 2316 (4300) | 303 (485) | 497 |
| `/walk-for-education/take-part/` | 3172 (3700) | 354 (410) | 227 |
| `/contribute/` | 1388 (3000) | 178 (460) | 148 |
| `/faith-in-motion/` | 3836 (4400) | 731 (840) | 395 |
| `/give/` | 2908 (3400) | 465 (535) | 200 |

The target is LCP 2.5 s, 450 KB and 200 ms blocking time. Pages over target today: `/about/` (2.5 s, on the line), `/projects/`, `/walk-for-education/take-part/`, `/faith-in-motion/` and `/give/` on LCP; `/faith-in-motion/` and `/give/` on weight; most pages on blocking time. The Walk home measures well under its ceiling here. The test loads the page and waits 3.5 s without scrolling, so images far down the page that load lazily are not counted. The ceilings should be ratcheted down to these results (plus a small margin) before the speed work starts, so that regressions are caught at the real level.

## 4. Responsive findings

Measured at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1536 px, phone landscape (667 x 375), and with text at 200% on a 390 px and a 1280 px screen. 30 pages.

| Viewport | Pages that scroll sideways | Pages with clipped content | Taps under 24 px that crowd another target | Taps under 44 px | Pages with text under 13 px |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 320 | 2 | 2 | 0 | 308 | 30 |
| 360 | 2 | 2 | 0 | 327 | 30 |
| 390 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 368 | 30 |
| 768 to 1536 | 0 | 0 | 0 | not measured (phone only) | 30 |
| Landscape | 0 | 0 | 1 | 388 | 30 |
| 200% text, 390 px | 18 | 16 | 0 | 105 | 14 |
| 200% text, 1280 px | 13 | 13 | 0 | 0 | 14 |

What fails, in order of how much a visitor would notice:

1. **Contribute privacy and terms scroll sideways on real phones** (49 px at 320, 9 px at 360). The cause is the page's `nav` element, which runs from 227 to 369 px on a 320 px screen.
2. **Every institutional page fails at 200% text.** At 390 px the menu button is pushed off screen (313 to 463 px) and the page scrolls sideways by 73 px; at 1280 px the dark button in the header runs 179 px past the edge.
3. **Walk home at 200% text on a phone** scrolls sideways by 157 px: the tier table (16 to 547 px) and a gallery image run past the edge. **Walk updates** by 130 px, **Walk partners** by 40 px, **Contribute payment** by 83 px.
4. **Walk home in phone landscape**: one link (146 x 23 px) is under 24 px high with another target close beside it.
5. **Text under 13 px on all 30 pages.** The common cases are eyebrow labels (11.5 px on 13 pages), small spans (11.2 px on 13 pages) and breadcrumbs (12 px).
6. **27 of 30 pages lack `viewport-fit=cover`.** Only `/walk-for-education/`, `/contribute/` and `/contribute/payment/` have it.
7. **12 pages have images without `width` and `height`** (34 images at 390 px; 19 of them on `/faith-in-motion/`). These can make the page jump while loading.
8. **Stylesheets use 37 distinct breakpoint widths**, 35 of them outside the decided 640, 900 and 1200. `100vh` is used in `css/wfe-campaign.css` (once) and `src/styles/base.css` (once).

The full per-page, per-viewport data is produced by `node tests/quality/responsive.cjs --json out.json`. Screenshots of the 11 key pages at the eight viewports are produced by adding `--shots <folder>`.
