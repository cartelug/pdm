The wrapper is now layout-transparent and the focal point is `58% 42%`, which keeps the full building, the bag and natural head-to-body proportions. Phone layout is unchanged.
- **Homepage hero, 720 px and up.** The headline was sized at up to 13% of its column and ran across the walker's face and kit. It is now 9.5% (max 6.5 rem), so it stays on the shaded side. Phone sizing is unchanged.
- **Staff start page.** The link read "View contributions"; the console button is "Review payments". The page now matches.
- **Joshua's guide** (`docs/joshua/`) aligned with the packaged ten-minute guide.

Generated pages and the compiled stylesheet were rebuilt. Three protected Walk pages changed only in the `official-kit.css` cache-hash query; each has a narrow review entry that keeps its previous hash.
