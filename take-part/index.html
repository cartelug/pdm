Faith in Motion / Take part

You don’t need money to carry this.

Four ways to take part, and every one of them moves the walk forward. Pick whichever is actually yours to give.

Ways to take part

01 · GIVE

Sponsor steps

One step is UGX 1,000 and claims 74 centimetres of a real road. You are told exactly which stretch is yours, and a published supporter label is recorded against it where supplied.

Choose your steps → 02 · WALK

Walk a stretch with him

Join the road for a kilometre or a morning. Families, parishes and clubs are welcome, subject to route safety on the day.

Ask to join a stretch → 03 · PRAY

Pray for the road

Send an intention for the walker, the parish and the work still to finish. Intentions are carried to the church at Rwembyo.

Send an intention → 04 · HOST

Host a collection

Run a collection in your parish, office, club or family group. You will be sent the number, a QR code and a short brief to read out.

Request a collection pack →

Send it to one person.

The cheapest thing anyone can do, and often the most valuable. One message to somebody who would carry a hundred steps.

Trust & transparency

Verify first. Then contribute.

01

Request current details

Transfer instructions and the beneficiary name are provided on request through the campaign enquiry contact.

02

Received means received

Pledges and promises remain visible commitments. Only verified received funds count toward public progress.

03

Verify before sending

Use the WhatsApp contact to request the current approved transfer details and beneficiary name. Treat it as an enquiry channel, not a payment channel.
