The walk

One man, one country, one church.

Our brother Shyaka — the Walking Rotarian — crossed Uganda on foot, from Akright City in Kampala to Kiburara in the Rwenzori foothills of Kasese. It was not his first long walk for a cause. Every kilometre was meant to move people who will never see the road, but who can help finish what waits at the end of it.
