How a project opens

Every Pamodzi project follows the same route from an idea to a live, fundable door. There is no fixed calendar — a project opens the moment it is verified, not before.

Step 01 · Proposed

Someone brings the work

A parish, school, association or local initiative describes a defined piece of work and who it serves. Nothing is announced yet.

Step 02 · Verified

Beneficiary, account and scope confirmed

The authorised account to receive funds, the scope of the work and the approvals are confirmed in writing. This is the step that protects everyone who gives.

Step 03 · Opened on the foundation

A profile goes live

The project inherits the design, the contact routing and the reporting discipline already built here — days of work, not months, because nothing starts from zero.

Step 04 · Reported until finished

Reconciled figures, all the way through

Progress and verified figures run on the updates feed until the work is complete — and stay on the record after it is done.

What counts as a community project

Pamodzi considers work that can be verified, reported on and actually finished. If your initiative fits the marks below, tell us about it — the gateway is built to open new doors quickly.

  • 01A defined piece of workA clear scope with an end — something that can be called finished, not an open-ended fund.
  • 02An authorised account to receive fundsMoney goes directly to a formally authorised account. Pamodzi and the campaign team never receive or hold a donation.
  • 03Something verifiableBeneficiary, need and progress can be confirmed and reported on — so only reconciled figures are ever published.
  • 04A community behind itWork carried by the people it serves, the way St Joseph's parish carried its church most of the way itself.
