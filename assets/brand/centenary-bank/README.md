# Centenary Bank mark

`centenary-bank-logo.png` identifies the bank transfer channel used for Faith in Motion contributions (Centenary Bank Kasese, Account 3205586406, St Joseph Rwembyo Catholic Church). Used as a small trust badge next to the payment details on the campaign pages and in PDF exports from the admin console.

The original supplied file had a solid white background; it has been keyed out to transparency (with edge colour decontamination, not just a hard cutout) so the mark drops cleanly onto both the light and dark payment-detail surfaces it's placed on.

Do not stretch, recolour, or crop the mark.
