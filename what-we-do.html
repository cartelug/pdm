PCI Uganda / What we do

Four pillars,
one method.

Every pillar runs on the same approach: convene the community, build the partnership, mobilise local resources, invest together — and keep the asset working long after it is built.

Pillar I · Education

Mobilising investment for quality education.

We convene communities, Government, the private sector, financial institutions and development partners to mobilise local resources and investment for sustainable education development.

Communities identify their own education priorities and take part in planning, financing, developing and maintaining the result. We work in collaboration with the Ministry of Education and Sports and District Local Governments.

  • →Schools and classroom blocksConstruction and rehabilitation.
  • →Science and ICT laboratoriesPlus libraries and learning resources.
  • →Teachers' housing and sanitationThe facilities that keep staff in post.
  • →Digital learning and teacher capacityInstitutional strengthening and public-private partnerships.
Pillar II · Health

Resilient, people-centred health systems.

We mobilise local capital, strategic partnerships and institutional collaboration to improve healthcare infrastructure, medical equipment and service delivery.

In partnership with the Ministry of Health and District Local Governments, communities, healthcare institutions, financial partners and professional associations collectively finance health initiatives that respond to local priorities.

  • →Health Centre II, III and IV facilitiesHospitals and maternity wards.
  • →Theatres and diagnostic laboratoriesPlus staff accommodation.
  • →Equipment and ambulancesModern medical equipment and transport.
  • →Energy, water and digital healthRenewable systems, WASH and health-worker development.
Pillar III · Climate & green development

Locally led climate solutions.

We mobilise communities, institutions and investors to finance climate solutions that protect ecosystems while creating sustainable economic opportunity.

Community ownership of climate investment is the point — partnerships accelerate conservation, renewable energy and climate-smart development, contributing to Uganda's climate and sustainable development commitments.

  • →Renewable energySolar, mini-hydro and biogas systems.
  • →Climate-smart agricultureIrrigation and water resource management.
  • →Afforestation and restorationLandscape restoration and green infrastructure.
  • →Waste management and educationEnvironmental education in communities.
Pillar IV · Financial inclusion & community enterprise

The foundation of the whole model.

We strengthen community-owned financial systems that increase access to capital, promote entrepreneurship and accelerate sustainable wealth creation.

Professionally governed investment platforms turn community savings into productive investment — financing enterprise, infrastructure, agriculture, affordable housing, renewable energy and other income-generating assets.

  • →VSLAs, SACCOs and cooperativesStrengthening existing community structures.
  • →Youth and women's enterprisesEnterprise development and peer support.
  • →Financial literacy and investment readinessBusiness incubation and digital financial services.
  • →Responsible reinvestmentReturns that expand community programmes.
