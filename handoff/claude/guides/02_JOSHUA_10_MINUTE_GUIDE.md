# Joshua's ten-minute start guide

You use a browser on your phone or computer. You do not install software or edit the website code.

Start page: https://pamodzici.com/admin/operations/start.html

Daily console: https://pamodzici.com/admin/operations/

Full staff guide: https://pamodzici.com/admin/operations/guide.html

## Before the ten minutes begin

Ask the PCI account administrator for your personal **coordinator** login. If you have not received an activated username and password, the administrator must complete guide 03 first. The website release did not automatically activate named accounts.

Bookmark the console on your phone. Your own account works on both phone and computer. Finance uses a different role to verify money; your coordinator account handles recording, reports, walking updates and graphics.

## Minutes 0–2: sign in and find four actions

Open the console and sign in. Locate:

- Record contribution.
- Update walk status.
- Generate graphic.
- View records / Download report.

A coordinator should not have a Verify & reconcile action. If you see unexpected permissions, ask the administrator to check your role rather than using them.

## Minutes 2–4: learn how to record a genuine contribution

Use the first real contribution you are authorised to record. If you have no real receipt, review the fields without saving a practice entry.

1. Enter the supporter, amount, currency, payment channel and transaction/reference details.
2. Check the actual date. New entries start with today's Uganda date, but an older receipt must keep its real date.
3. If the receipt pays part of an existing pledge, choose that pledge instead of making a duplicate.
4. Check the stored country/step price. An older pledge keeps its historical price; do not replace it with today's price.
5. Save once. It is awaiting finance verification, not automatically confirmed money.

If a gateway payment already exists in the online ledger, ask finance to review it rather than entering it again as a separate manual receipt. Search the reference before repeating an entry.

## Minutes 4–5: understand what finance does

Finance matches the entry to the actual bank/mobile-money receipt and settlement, then reconciles it. Only eligible verified money increases public received collections. A pledge, WhatsApp promise or screenshot alone is not a reconciled receipt.

Keep these four things separate: promised support, received money, sponsored steps and kilometres walked. Uganda and Kenya amounts also remain in their own currencies.

## Minutes 5–7: prepare an approved walking update

Open Update walk status. Use the walker/team's confirmed date, town, cumulative kilometres, short story and approved photo. The walk is already underway, but that does not supply today's location or distance.

Preview the update. Publish only the approved facts, or keep a draft. Campaign activity and the walker's physical state are separate: an ongoing campaign can have a rest day. Never invent a new stage or reset cumulative distance to fit a graphic.

## Minutes 7–9: practise a graphic safely

Choose **Generate graphic → SAMPLE DATA → Collections snapshot → Feed → Create graphic preview → Download PNG**.

This is the safe practice route. SAMPLE DATA graphics do not write a donation into the register. For a real post, select the approved live snapshot instead and check its reporting cut-off. Supporter names and amounts need the appropriate consent before recognition.

Open the downloaded PNG on your phone. Confirm that the logos, wording and numbers are readable and the image is complete. Previewing the page is not the same as checking the downloaded file.

## Minutes 9–10: check and sign out

Open https://pamodzici.com/walk-for-education/ and check the published update. Public views refresh on a new page load or within about a minute on an open page. Find Download report in the console, then sign out on a shared device.

## The standard daily routine

1. Record each genuine contribution once.
2. Finance checks and reconciles it.
3. Publish the approved walking update when the walker/team supplies it.
4. Review pending entries with finance, download the report and generate an approved graphic when needed.
5. Sign out.

## If something looks wrong

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot sign in | Confirm your own activated account with the administrator; do not share another person's login |
| You saved the wrong amount or reference | Give finance the entry/reference so it can correct it through the audited process; do not add a second entry to cancel it |
| The total did not rise after saving | Check whether finance has reconciled the receipt |
| The service is unavailable | Wait and retry; unavailable does not mean collections are zero |
| A supporter withdraws recognition consent | Ask finance to update consent; remove previously shared graphics manually as well |
| A location or kilometre value is uncertain | Save a draft or wait for confirmation |

This written walkthrough is a training aid. Completion means Joshua has actually signed in, found the four actions, downloaded a sample PNG and understood the finance handoff; sending the guide alone does not prove training is complete.
