## Facts and conflicts to handle honestly

`js/wfe-data.js` is the canonical static campaign record, and the public approved operations projection supplies live facts. The five-year ambition is UGX 25 billion. It is not the same thing as an approved short-term operational milestone or money received.

The original printed shirt includes 1,300 km and 1,650,000 steps, while the static route-planning source currently carries approximately 1,100 km. Keep original artwork intact and flag this discrepancy for an approved factual decision. Neither value establishes kilometres already walked. Sponsored steps are contribution units and do not measure physical walking distance.

Do not seed public totals from a WhatsApp list, add up unlike currencies, treat an old spreadsheet “paid” label as finance reconciliation, or invent a report cut-off. If importing historical records is requested, use the original register, preview its mapping, preserve source row identity and dates, resolve duplicates and have finance verify evidence.

Payment instructions already in the public source are part of the existing approved UI. Do not copy private payment credentials into the redesign, change account numbers from memory, or replace the tested forms with a decorative button.

## Deliberate design decisions

- Archivo headings/UI/figures and Lexend reading/forms; no public italics in the current system.
- PCI paper/crimson and Walk navy/ivory/gold/cyan share one type/token foundation.
- Walk is the first project priority; Faith in Motion follows; apartments is not a promoted live concept.
- Main university block is the signature architecture, as specified in the prior Joshua reference.
- Phone images are independently composed, not just landscape crops.
- Useful motion respects reduced motion and no-JavaScript access; no blocking splash screen.
- Protected financial and pledge behaviour survives a visual redesign.
- Existing public URLs and relative asset portability are preserved.

## Work that is optional, not a hidden unfinished requirement

Migrating handwritten campaign pages into Astro is a separate, page-by-page project requiring hook/fragment equivalence and rollback. Moving the heavy gallery to a separate route is an optional product decision, not necessary to use the current gallery. Replacing the font families or changing hosting is a deliberate new scope. None of those should be bundled casually into the next visual refinement.

No new site change was made to create this handoff. Claude should first inspect the supplied release, then implement Neeza's next requested improvement rather than automatically redoing shipped work.
