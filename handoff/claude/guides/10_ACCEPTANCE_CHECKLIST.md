# Completion checklist for the next Claude release

Use this against the actual final result, not a plan or screenshot alone.

## Product and content

- The first view explains the live walk, educational purpose, destination and useful next action.
- Walk for Education is the first campaign; Faith in Motion and the other work follow correctly.
- Unknown date/location/km/collections stay unknown until approved evidence supplies them.
- Actual kit/face/main-building references are preserved; arrival illustrations are labelled.
- No fabricated donor names, money, progress or partner confirmation is published.

## Design and fonts

- Archivo and Lexend roles are consistent across all public page families, or a deliberate owner-approved replacement is fully documented.
- Real headings wrap naturally on phones, body copy has a comfortable measure, forms remain legible and currency figures align.
- Gradients protect readability without hiding important image subjects.
- Phone and desktop each load their intended image composition; decorative participation art remains small.
- Menus, fixed controls, landscape layouts and 200% text work without clipping or horizontal overflow.
- Keyboard, focus, reduced motion and no-JavaScript behaviour are checked.

## Functional continuity

- Pledge, contribution, country price, referral, bounds, payment, certificate and enquiry contracts remain intact.
- Coordinators record; finance reconciles; public sums use the approved projection and separate currencies.
- Consent, partial pledges, duplicate prevention, imports, reports and audit history remain tested.
- PNG verification checks the actual downloaded file, including long text/amount cases and current consent handling.
- Staff accounts are reported active only after host activation and a real role check.

## Code and evidence

- Real remote history is preserved; no force-push or blind snapshot overwrite.
- Source and generated output agree; compiled assets are not hand-edited.
- Protected exceptions retain their previous hashes and narrow review records.
- `npm run validate`, required backend/browser tests, axe, speed and strict responsive checks pass without relaxed gates.
- The final diff contains no private credentials, live ledger, test uploads or accidental generated scratch files.

## Publishing and handover

- Publish only according to Neeza's current instruction.
- Observe all required workflows for the exact final commit, not an older successful run.
- Verify both addresses, representative images, manifest/gallery and the intended backend availability/state.
- State what changed, how it was tested, the commit/live link and any genuine remaining dependency.
- Joshua can complete the ten-minute walkthrough from his phone, using sample graphics safely and understanding the finance handoff.
