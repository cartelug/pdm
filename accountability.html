Verification

How a shilling becomes a published figure.

01

It arrives directly

Funds go to the church's authorised construction account. Neither the campaign team nor THE 97 ever receives or holds a donation.

02

It is reconciled

The designated finance verifier matches each payment to a contributor and a status before anything is recorded as received.

03

Then it is published

Only reconciled figures reach the site. The Roll of Honour, the totals and the road all update from that one verified ledger.
