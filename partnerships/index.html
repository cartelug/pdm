Partnerships

A way in for
every partner.

Partnership pathways for institutions, businesses, funders, technical specialists and community organisations working toward locally led development.

The approach

Structured, not ad hoc.

Pamodzi begins with local priorities and resources, then builds the relationships, technical support and appropriate funding route needed for a defined development initiative.

These partnerships leverage local knowledge, community ownership, innovation and professional expertise to develop scalable solutions aligned with Uganda’s national development priorities and the Sustainable Development Goals.

We are committed to transparent, accountable and mutually beneficial partnerships founded on trust, shared responsibility and measurable impact.
Our partnership ecosystem

Four routes into the work.

  1. 01

    Government institutions and agencies

    We welcome dialogue with public institutions and local governments where a community-led initiative needs policy alignment, technical guidance or coordination with public services.

    Policy alignmentPublic service deliveryTechnical guidanceLocal planning
  2. 02

    Development and financing partners

    Potential collaboration with development agencies, financial institutions and philanthropic organisations can bring technical expertise, programme funding and responsible financial support.

    Sustainable infrastructureClimate-resilient investmentEnterprise developmentFinancial inclusionInstitutional strengthening
  3. 03

    Private sector and civil society

    We welcome businesses, professional associations, civil society, faith-based organisations, academic institutions and research bodies that can contribute knowledge, networks, practical support or programme resources.

    Corporate social investmentTechnical expertiseInnovation solutionsCommunity engagementKnowledge & research
  4. 04

    Community and local development partners

    At the centre of the model are communities themselves. The work starts with existing local structures so that people identify priorities, mobilise resources and take part in decisions.

    Savings groupsCooperativesInvestment groupsAlumni associationsFaith-based groupsRotary & Lions ClubsProfessional networksCBOs
Organisational support

Help strengthen the foundation.

We welcome conversations with grantmakers, businesses, philanthropists and development partners interested in strengthening Pamodzi’s organisational capacity and community programmes.

The right route depends on the purpose of the support, the parties involved and the documentation required. Those details are agreed directly before any commitment is made.

Clear terms for every route. Grants, donations, sponsorships, membership contributions and any commercial investment are separate. No investment or financial terms are offered on this website.

Grants Programme and capacity support
Sponsorship Support for a defined activity or campaign
Partnership Expertise, networks and shared delivery

Walking for Impact

Turning participation into financing.

Walking for Impact is a campaign model designed to connect public participation to a defined, independently authorised community project.

Faith in Motion and Walk for Education are separate campaigns. Each has its own beneficiary, source documents, contribution route and reconciliation record.

  • 01 A champion walks A designated journey for a defined cause.
  • 02 Each step carries a value The journey becomes a measurable target.
  • 03 Supporters sponsor steps Individuals, families, businesses and institutions.
  • 04 Funds reach the project Linked directly to the identified development need.
