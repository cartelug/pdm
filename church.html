The church

A parish finishing what faith started.

St Joseph Rwembyo stands at Kiburara, Kisinga, in the Rwenzori foothills of Kasese. The congregation has carried the building most of the way on its own — raising walls and roofing it without waiting for anyone. The walk exists to close the final gap and turn a standing structure into a finished church.
