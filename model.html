PCI Uganda / Our model

The community as investor.

Our Integrated Community Investment and Asset Development Model — ICIADM — places communities at the centre of financing and implementing their own development priorities.

The idea

Rather than wait for donors, organise what is already here.

PCI Uganda acts as convener, catalyst and strategic mobiliser — bringing together individuals, community groups, financial institutions, private sector organisations, professional associations, faith-based organisations, local governments and development partners to invest collectively.

The model rests on a simple belief: lasting development happens when communities are able to mobilise their own resources, build strategic partnerships and take collective ownership of the solution. So we organise and strengthen legally constituted community investment platforms that mobilise domestic capital, technical expertise and institutional support.

As a trusted intermediary we connect communities with government, financial institutions, private partners and development agencies — turning fragmented local resources into coordinated investment. We work with the structures that already exist: savings groups, cooperatives, alumni associations, Rotary and Lions Clubs, faith-based organisations, business associations, investment clubs and professional networks.

PCI Uganda is not simply implementing projects — it is building self-reliant communities.

Beyond community projects

Investment platforms that keep earning.

PCI Uganda also facilitates professionally governed investment platforms that let members pool resources, acquire productive assets, generate sustainable income and reinvest returns into expanding community development programmes.

This turns community savings and private capital into structured investment ecosystems capable of financing education, healthcare, agriculture, renewable energy, affordable housing and enterprise development — sectors that contribute directly to Uganda's national development priorities.

Governance first. Central to the model is a framework built on transparency, accountability, professional management and responsible stewardship of collective resources. This safeguards community assets and builds the confidence partners need.
