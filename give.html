How it works
  1. Choose your steps. One step is UGX 1,000. Give twenty, a hundred, a thousand — any number at all.
  2. Send by Mobile Money to the construction line, using your own name as the reference.
  3. Confirm on WhatsApp so the gift is verified and added to the Roll of Honour.
  4. Watch the road fill. The gold line moves as confirmed gifts come in.

Contributions go directly to the church's authorised construction account. The campaign team never receives or holds your donation, and only reconciled figures are published on this page. The number above is the only official channel — treat any other request as fraudulent.
