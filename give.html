How it works
  1. Choose your steps. One step is UGX 1,000. Sponsor twenty, a hundred, a thousand — any number at all.
  2. Request the details. Ask the campaign contact for the current approved transfer instructions and beneficiary name.
  3. Verify before sending. Confirm that the beneficiary and instructions match the Construction Committee's current approval.
  4. Wait for reconciliation. The gold line moves only when a contribution is received or formally pledged and checked.

The WhatsApp number above is an enquiry contact, not a published payment channel. Do not send funds to any number or account until you have requested the current approved transfer details and verified the beneficiary name.
