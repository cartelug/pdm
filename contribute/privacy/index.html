Contribution information

Your details, handled carefully.

What this form records

Your name, phone or email, campaign, amount, contribution type and any campaign referral. For payments, PCI records provider references, verified status, receipt, sponsored steps, certificate number and finance reconciliation information.

Why these records are needed

To process or follow up your support, help with payment questions, issue a payment record and personalised contribution certificate, reconcile collections and maintain an accountable campaign register. A pledge may be followed up using the contact details you provide.

Payment details

Payment methods are presented by Pesapal. This website does not request or store your mobile-money PIN or full card details. Your relevant contact and contribution information is sent to Pesapal when a payment request is made.

Access and publication

Authorised PCI administrators and finance personnel can access contribution records. The form does not publish your name or contact details. Public progress uses aggregate, reconciled figures. Certificate-number verification shows the campaign, step count, contribution amount and issue date without your name or contact details. The certificate you download includes the name you provided. Separate consent is needed for public acknowledgement.

Questions and corrections

Contact PCI through its contact page to ask about your record or request a correction. Keep your contribution reference. The campaign team must confirm any retention or deletion arrangements that apply to financial records.
