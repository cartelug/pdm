Pledges and payments

A pledge expresses an intention to support. It is not a completed payment, a receipt for funds received or an addition to the public received total.

Sponsored steps and certificates

One sponsored step has a fixed price in the currency of the country you choose, for example UGX 5,000 in Uganda or KSh 200 in Kenya. The minimum contribution is 5 steps. Choose whole steps; your contribution amount is calculated before checkout. A personalised contribution certificate becomes downloadable after a verified successful live payment. Pledges, test, pending and failed payments do not receive a certificate. Reversed payments make the certificate inactive. A contribution certificate recognises support; it is not an academic award or tax certificate.

Secure payment handoff

Online payment opens only when PCI has approved the checkout. Your amount is stated in the currency of your country. Until PCI confirms a currency with Pesapal, contributions in it are recorded as pledges and the team sends payment details. Pesapal presents the payment methods available for PCI and any applicable checkout information.

Confirmation and public progress

A successful payment record follows server verification with the provider. PCI finance separately matches verified payments to settlement records. Public received figures use reconciled funds; pledges and pending payments are shown separately.

Payment problems, reversals and refund enquiries

If payment fails, remains pending or shows a reversal, retain your payment reference and contact PCI before paying again. Contact PCI for any refund request. This page does not promise an automatic refund or a processing deadline; PCI must approve its detailed refund terms before live checkout opens.

Campaign purpose

Walk for Education mobilises support for UCU–Kagando University College and the neighbouring nursery and primary school. Its UGX 25 billion target describes the five-year vision. Specific institutional sponsorship commitments are agreed separately with PCI.
