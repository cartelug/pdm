Can I support without paying today?

Yes. A pledge records an intended number of steps and your contact details. No money is collected and no paid certificate is issued.

Does the WhatsApp message send automatically?

No. Your pledge is saved on the website first. WhatsApp opens with your message ready, and you tap Send to share it with the campaign team.

Will my name appear publicly?

No. Names and contact details remain private. Certificate verification confirms the payment and steps without displaying your name.
