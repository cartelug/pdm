Can I support without paying today?

Yes. A pledge records an intended number of steps and your contact details. No money is collected and no paid certificate is issued.

What if payment is pending?

Keep the payment result page and check its status before paying again. A pending payment does not receive a certificate. Contact the team with your reference if you need help.

Does my certificate arrive by email?

You download your certificate directly from the verified payment result page. Automatic email delivery is not active yet.

Will my name appear publicly?

No. Names and contact details remain private. Certificate verification confirms the payment and steps without displaying your name.
