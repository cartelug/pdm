Can I support without paying today?

Yes. A pledge records an intended amount and your contact details. It does not collect money or count toward received funds.

What does my contribution support?

The campaign mobilises support for university expansion, student accommodation, teaching and ICT facilities, and Kagando Nursery & Primary School. The UGX 25 billion figure is the five-year campaign target.

What if payment is still pending?

Keep the payment result page and check its status before paying again. Contact the team with your reference if you need help.

Will my name appear publicly?

No. This form does not publish names or contact details. Public acknowledgement requires separate consent.
