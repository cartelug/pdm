PCI Uganda / Partnerships

A way in for
every partner.

Sustainable development happens when communities, institutions and partners work together to mobilise resources, share expertise and take collective ownership of the solution.

The approach

Structured, not ad hoc.

Rather than relying primarily on external funding, PCI Uganda mobilises domestic resources, community contributions, institutional partnerships, technical expertise and shared investment to support the planning, financing, implementation and sustainability of development initiatives.

These partnerships leverage local knowledge, community ownership, innovation and professional expertise to develop scalable solutions aligned with Uganda's national development priorities and the Sustainable Development Goals.

We are committed to transparent, accountable and mutually beneficial partnerships founded on trust, shared responsibility and measurable impact.

Founding capital framework

Helping build the foundation.

PCI Uganda is establishing a Founding Capital Framework for strategic partners, investors, philanthropists and development champions who want to help build the institution's initial capital foundation.

Participation is capped deliberately — between one and five founding positions per partner — to keep capital mobilisation disciplined, governance strong and institutional focus intact.

An illustrative framework, not an offer. The structure below is indicative and is shared for discussion only. It is not a public investment offer, and no financial return is offered or implied. Walking for Impact

Turning participation into financing.

Our Walking for Impact campaigns monetise every step taken by designated champions, community ambassadors and development advocates — connecting public participation directly to a defined development project.

The model has been applied to education infrastructure, school rehabilitation, faith-based community projects, community infrastructure, social welfare and environmental conservation. Faith in Motion — the 345 km walk for St Joseph Catholic Church at Rwembyo — is the live demonstration.
