Who we are

A platform built around local initiative.

Pamodzi mobilises relationships, local resources and practical expertise around priorities chosen by communities themselves, with programme interests spanning education, health, climate resilience and financial inclusion.

Our model is designed to connect communities with public institutions, development partners, businesses and professional networks when a defined piece of work needs shared effort.

What distinguishes the platform is not any single project but the method behind the work: communities participate in setting priorities, organising resources and taking responsibility for what follows.

Our vision

Communities across Uganda mobilising partnerships, local resources, innovation and practical expertise to shape their own sustainable development.

Our mission

To catalyse sustainable community transformation by mobilising partnerships, investment, innovation and technical expertise to deliver high-impact programmes across Uganda.

Strategic objectives

What we are committed to.

  • 01Quality education infrastructureImproving access to conducive learning environments and modern education facilities.
  • 02Stronger health systemsModern infrastructure, medical equipment and enhanced service delivery.
  • 03Climate resilienceRenewable energy, environmental conservation and climate-smart agriculture.
  • 04Expanded financial inclusionIncreasing access to finance, investment capital and entrepreneurship opportunities.
  • 05Institutional capacity and governanceStrengthening partnerships to deliver sustainable and measurable development outcomes.
