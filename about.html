Pamodzi Community Initiative is a nationally focused, indigenous development institution — established in 2022 to mobilise partnerships, financing and technical expertise for community transformation.

Who we are

An institution built for local capital.

PCI Uganda is dedicated to mobilising partnerships, financing and technical expertise to transform communities through sustainable investments in education, healthcare, climate resilience and financial inclusion.

We work closely with Government ministries, local governments, development partners, private sector institutions and communities themselves to deliver high-impact infrastructure, institutional capacity building and community empowerment programmes across Uganda.

What distinguishes the institution is not any single project but the method behind all of them: communities are treated as investors in their own future, not as beneficiaries of someone else's plan.
