Pamodzi / About

Pamodzi means together.

Not a slogan — a method. One foundation shared by many projects, so that every finished piece of work makes the next one easier to trust and easier to fund.

Who we are

Built to finish things.

Pamodzi for Development builds and backs projects across Uganda — places of worship, homes and community work. What makes it different is not any single project but the discipline around all of them: verified before announced, funded through authorised accounts, reported until done.

DO

What we do

Open, run and report on development projects — from a church completion campaign to a residential development — under one trusted address.

DON'T

What we never do

Hold donations in personal hands, publish unverified figures, or promise outcomes. The rules are the same for every project, every time.

WHY

Why a gateway

Standalone campaigns rebuild trust from zero. Shared foundations let trust compound — each finished project vouches for the next.

The method

Why a gateway, not a website per project.

Every campaign that stands alone rebuilds trust from zero — new page, new channels, new doubts. Pamodzi’s projects share one address, one reporting discipline and one contact routing, so each finished project makes the next one easier to fund. Trust compounds; websites don’t.
