Pamodzi for Development is a community-led Ugandan platform that brings members and partners together for mutual support, resource mobilisation and sustainable local development.

Who we are

A platform built around local initiative.

Pamodzi mobilises relationships, local resources and practical expertise around priorities chosen by communities themselves, with programme interests spanning education, health, climate resilience and financial inclusion.

Our model is designed to connect communities with public institutions, development partners, businesses and professional networks when a defined piece of work needs shared effort.

What distinguishes the platform is not any single project but the method behind the work: communities participate in setting priorities, organising resources and taking responsibility for what follows.
