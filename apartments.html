What happens after you register

A single, predictable route from registering interest to sitting down together. Nothing unverified is shared, and there is no obligation at any step.

Step 01 · You register

Send an expression of interest

Tell us who you are and what you'd like to explore, on the channel that suits you. This registers interest only — never a commitment.

Step 02 · We make contact

A reply within a few days

We come back to you on the channel you gave, to understand what you're looking for and answer first questions.

Step 03 · Documents shared directly

Concept material and confirmed status

Approved project documents are shared with you directly — concept material and verified status, nothing speculative or unconfirmed.

Step 04 · Meeting or site visit

Sit down, in Kasese or Kampala

Arranged at a time that works for you, in person or online, to take the conversation as far as you'd like it to go.
