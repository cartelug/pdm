For Rotary clubs

Carry the
relay forward.

Support the Walking Rotarian, Rtn. Shyaka Mbanda, through fellowship, mobilisation and local connections. Your club can help in seven ways.

  1. Receive the Walking Rotarian.
  2. Host a fellowship or campaign presentation.
  3. Mobilise Steps for Education.
  4. Discuss becoming a Builder + Steps Partner.
  5. Bring in community and corporate networks.
  6. Coordinate walking part of the route.
  7. Connect the relay to the next club.
