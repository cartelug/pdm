Know the difference

Support takes
different forms.

A pledge, a successful payment and a finance-reconciled received total each describe a different point in the process.

01 · PLEDGED

An intention
to support.

Your chosen steps and contact details are recorded. No money is collected and no paid certificate is issued.

02 · PAYMENT VERIFIED

A confirmed
transaction.

Pesapal confirms a successful live payment. Your private result page makes the receipt and contribution certificate available.

03 · RECONCILED

A received
public figure.

PCI finance reconciles the payment. Only these live digital payments enter the public digital received total.

In-kind support stays separate.

Equipment, materials and expertise are recorded separately from cash unless an approved valuation policy applies.
