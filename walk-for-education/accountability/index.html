Know the difference

Support takes
different forms.

A pledge, a reported contribution and a finance-verified received total each describe a different point in the process.

01 · PLEDGED

An intention
to support.

Your chosen steps and contact details are recorded. No money is collected and no paid certificate is issued.

02 · PAYMENT REPORTED

A reported
contribution.

You contribute through an approved external channel and send the transaction reference privately to the campaign team.

03 · RECONCILED

A received
public figure.

PCI finance checks and reconciles the actual receipt. Only eligible verified receipts enter the public collected total.

In-kind support stays separate.

Equipment, materials and expertise are recorded separately from cash unless an approved valuation policy applies.
