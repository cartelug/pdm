Clarity before your contribution

A few things
you may ask.

Need a hand or contributing as a group?

Talk to the campaign team ↗
What does one step cost?

One sponsored step is UGX 5,000. Choose one step or any whole number of steps. For example, 10 steps are UGX 50,000 and 100 steps are UGX 500,000.

Can I make a pledge before payments open?

Yes. The contribution page lets you pledge your chosen steps and leave your contact details. A pledge records your intention to support; no money is collected and no paid contribution certificate is issued.

When will I receive my certificate?

Your certificate becomes available to download on your payment result page after the payment provider verifies a successful live payment. A return from checkout alone does not confirm payment. Keep the result page open in the browser you used to contribute.

What does my contribution support?

The campaign supports UCU–Kagando University College expansion and the neighbouring nursery & primary school. Priorities include student accommodation, learning spaces, ICT and campus infrastructure. UGX 25 billion is the five-year campaign goal.

How do I know my contribution is recorded?

Every contribution has a unique reference. Verified payments receive a receipt. Public received totals include only live digital payments reconciled by PCI finance, with pledges recorded separately. Read about campaign accountability.

Can my organisation become a sponsor?

Yes. Contact the campaign team to discuss support and recognition. Participation, sponsor status and any facility-specific arrangement are confirmed through a separate written agreement.
