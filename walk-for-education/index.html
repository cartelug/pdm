Why this walk

The road is the invitation.

The Walk for Education is mobilising sponsorships, partnerships and public support for a stronger education pathway in and around Kagando. The campaign focuses on expanding UCU–Kagando University College while strengthening the neighbouring nursery and primary school — connecting foundational learning, higher education and long-term community opportunity.

What the campaign will build

Five priorities. One education pathway.

The walk creates attention; the development programme turns that attention into facilities, learning capacity and room for sustainable growth.

Student Hostels

Increase residential capacity so more students, including those from rural and farming families, can study at UCU–Kagando.

Lecture Rooms, Library & ICT

Expand modern learning spaces, library resources, digital infrastructure and teaching facilities.

Faculty, Staff & Campus Infrastructure

Build the offices, staff accommodation and core facilities a growing university requires.

Kagando Nursery & Primary School

Strengthen the neighbouring foundational-learning environment for children and families in the surrounding community.

University Expansion

Build the infrastructure needed to support planned growth from approximately 802 students toward 4,000–5,000 over five years.
