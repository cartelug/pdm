The five-year UGX 25 billion mobilisation goal

Five priorities. A shared education future.

Direct your support to a specific need, or leave it with the overall campaign. Scope, budgets, designation and procurement are agreed with the campaign and institutional partners.

  1. 01

    Student hostels

    More room to live and learn.

    Increase residential capacity for students, including those travelling from rural and farming communities.

  2. 02

    Better spaces. Better tools.

    Expand teaching facilities, library resources and digital learning infrastructure.

    Discuss support ↗
  3. 03

    A stronger campus behind every learner.

    Support faculty offices, staff accommodation and essential facilities for a growing university.

    Discuss support ↗
  4. 04
