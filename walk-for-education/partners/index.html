Partnership routes

Bring the resource you can move.

The right route depends on the partner, the priority and the documentation required. Each commitment is agreed directly — no blanket benefit or commercial right is implied.

01

Financial sponsorship

Support the overall campaign or an approved, designated education priority.

02

Buildings & construction

Discuss materials, facilities, technical inputs or other documented capital support.

03

Equipment & materials

Support learning spaces, library resources, ICT and other approved facility needs.

04

Technical expertise

05

Programme investment

Develop a structured initiative aligned to the campaign's education-development priorities.

06

Research & innovation

Explore approved research, training, entrepreneurship or innovation partnerships.

Capital priorities

Where partnership can become infrastructure.

The source proposal identifies five development priorities. Scope, budget, procurement and governance are confirmed before any designated commitment is finalised.

Student Hostels

Accommodation capacity that widens access and supports students travelling from outside Kagando.

Learning Spaces & ICT

Lecture rooms, library resources, ICT systems and modern teaching facilities.

Faculty & Staff Infrastructure

Offices, staff accommodation and the operational facilities required for growth.

Nursery & Primary School

A stronger foundational-learning environment for the neighbouring community.

University Expansion

Planned infrastructure to support growth from approximately 802 students toward 4,000–5,000 over five years.

Recognition & safeguards

Visibility follows agreement.

Recognition depends on the agreed sponsorship and may include campaign branding, event visibility, media acknowledgement, finale recognition or recognition tied to a supported facility or initiative.

01 · DOCUMENTED

Written scope

Every material commitment has an agreed purpose, responsibilities and reporting route.

02 · APPROVED

Institutional control

Facility naming, procurement and commercial supply arrangements remain subject to separate approval.

03 · ACCURATE

Confirmed status

An invited organisation is never shown as a confirmed sponsor until written confirmation exists.
