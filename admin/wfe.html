Checking the live campaign record…

How this works. Anything you leave empty stays hidden on the public site. The moment you publish a verified value — a date, a payment channel, a reconciled total, a field note — the matching section appears on every Walk for Education page within seconds. Source-document facts (1,100 km, UGX 25B, student numbers) are fixed in the code and cannot be changed here.

1 · Unlock live publishing

Same password as the Faith in Motion console. It is remembered on this device only.

2 · Status & dates

Set dates only once they are confirmed for public coordination. A start date switches on the public countdown.

3 · Route & progress

Distance walked moves the marker along the public route line. Stages appear as a list, with reached stages marked.

4 · Contributions

A unit value switches on the public step calculator. A payment channel switches on the copy-number cards on Take Part and Accountability.

5 · Reconciled reporting

Only reconciled funds go in "Received". Pledges stay separate and are never added to the received total.

6 · Field notes

Newest first on the public Updates page; the latest also appears on the campaign page. Images: a path such as assets/walk-for-education/photo.jpg uploaded with the site, or an https link.

7 · Confirmed partners

Shown alongside the three source-document roles. Add an organisation only when participation and public acknowledgement are both confirmed in writing.

8 · What will be public after you publish

    Changes stay on this screen until you publish.
