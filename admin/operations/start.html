3. Joshua’s 10-minute practice

  1. Minutes 0–2 · Open and sign in. Find Record contribution, Update walk status, Generate graphic and Download report. Your coordinator login does not have a Verify & reconcile button.
  2. Minutes 2–4 · Understand a contribution. For the first real receipt, enter the supporter, amount, payment channel and reference. Check the currency and actual payment date, then save. Today’s date and the published Uganda/Kenya step price are supplied for new entries. Select a related pledge for a partial payment. Historical entries keep their own price; do not replace it with today’s price.
  3. Minute 4–5 · Understand verification. Saved means awaiting verification. Finance checks actual bank or mobile-money records and reconciles the receipt. Only then does it increase public received collections. A pledge or screenshot alone is not proof of receipt.
  4. Minutes 5–7 · Prepare a walk update. Use the confirmed town, date, cumulative kilometres, short story and approved photo. Keep campaign activity separate from whether the walker is moving or resting. Preview, then publish when approved. Save draft if you are not ready.
  5. Minutes 7–9 · Practise a graphic safely. Choose Generate graphic → SAMPLE DATA → Collections snapshot → Feed → Create graphic preview → Download PNG. This practice uses no contribution records. For real sharing, switch to Approved live snapshot.
  6. Minute 9–10 · Check and sign out. Open the public Walk page, check the dated update, and find Download report in the console. Public updates appear on refresh or within a minute. Sign out when finished.

Practice rule: use SAMPLE DATA graphics for rehearsal. Do not enter fake donations or publish an invented walk status in the live register.

4. The standard daily routine

  1. When a contribution is reported: the coordinator records it once and attaches the correct reference. Online gateway receipts stay in their existing ledger; ask finance instead of entering them again.
  2. Finance reviews: the verifier checks receipt, currency, amount and settlement, then reconciles. The coordinator never marks money as received.
  3. When the walker sends an approved update: publish the confirmed date, location, kilometres, story and photo.
  4. At the end of the day: review pending entries with finance, download the report and generate an approved graphic if needed. Only recognise supporters who have given the relevant permission.

Always separate: promised support, verified money, sponsored steps and kilometres walked.

If something is wrong: do not record it twice. Search the reference and ask finance to review. If the service is unavailable, wait and retry; it does not mean collections are zero.
