PCI campaign operations · staff guide

Sign in at /admin/operations/ with your own named account. Keep the login private and sign out when finished.

  1. Find a website pledge. Search its saved reference in Records & review. Website and staff entries appear together. Choose Record a payment for this pledge when a genuine payment is reported; do not re-enter the pledge because a WhatsApp message arrived.
  2. Record a contribution. Choose Record contribution. Enter contributor, whole amount, currency, date, channel and transaction reference. Select a related pledge for a partial payment. A pledge or reported payment adds nothing to public collections. Optional evidence and notes remain private.
  3. Verify a receipt (finance). Search in Review payments. Match the reference, amount and currency to actual bank/mobile-money records. Choose Verify & reconcile and record the evidence and settlement reference. Screenshots or “paid” claims alone do not establish receipt. Reconciliation adds the amount once. For mistakes, reverse with a reason and create a replacement; never delete history. Review Pesapal receipts in the gateway console.
  4. Share the latest update. Choose Download latest campaign update PDF. Share this branded public summary in the group; it excludes private supporter contacts and receipt evidence. Its cut-off identifies the saved snapshot. Visitors can also open Latest figures and download the current public PDF. Use Private editable PDF or Excel workbook only for authorised staff. Offline edits do not update the website.
  5. Find reports. Download report exports the operational register as CSV, including original source rows. The overview separates received, pending, pledged and outstanding by currency. The CSV includes manual and gateway rows; included_received identifies receipts counted by the aggregation policy. Never sum excluded overlaps or legacy aggregate totals.
  6. Import a register. Export CSV, retain a stable source name and original row order, map the columns and preview. Resolve every missing field and duplicate candidate before importing. Imported “paid” claims remain awaiting verification. Repeat imports are blocked as duplicates; changed historical source rows must be corrected through reversal and replacement.
  7. Publish a walking update (coordinator/owner/editor). Choose UPDATE WALK STATUS. Set campaign activity and physical walking state independently; enter confirmed cumulative kilometres, date, town, story and an approved photo plus description. Upload a phone photo or use an existing approved photo path. Preview, then publish. Connected pages refresh within a minute or on reload. Save draft keeps it private. Revision history can load a draft or republish a previous version as a new audited entry.
  8. Generate a graphic. Choose HD share studio. Select a template and format; choose SAMPLE DATA for practice or Approved live snapshot for publication. Preview, then download PNG. Financial figures and dates come from an immutable approved snapshot. A milestone graphic requires an approved milestone. A thank-you graphic checks current consent again at download. Story content stays in the central safe area.
  9. Recognition (authorised staff). Open Recognition / withdraw on a receipt. Record the supporter’s explicit permission separately for name and amount, with evidence. To withdraw, clear both boxes and record the request. Connected pages update on refresh; previously downloaded or posted images must be removed manually.

Keep distinct: walking kilometres, sponsored steps, received funds, pledges, outstanding pledge balances and remaining milestone balance. The five-year ambition is UGX 25 billion; the proposed UGX 25 million milestone remains unpublished until finance records approval.

Unavailable service? Stop recording and retry after the host owner restores service. An unavailable API is never a zero collection balance. This release records pledges and external contributions; online checkout remains outside its scope.
